About IntelliMOST

IntelliMOST is a production intelligence and MOST work study platform built for pharmaceutical and process manufacturing teams.

We help industrial engineers digitize standard time studies, give plant managers real-time visibility into OEE and downtime, and equip quality teams with structured CAPA and root cause workflows. AI assistance speeds up the routine work so your people can focus on improvement.
